## Analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count page
views and measure loading performance. Both are **cookieless**: they set nothing
on your device, do not track you between sites, and do not collect information
that identifies you. Vercel Inc. processes this data on our behalf.

No Google Analytics, no advertising trackers, no session recording, no
fingerprinting.

## Subscribing

If you subscribe, your email address is held by the email provider named on the
subscription form and used only to send new pieces. Unsubscribe from any email.

## Your rights

Smaddanki LTD is the data controller. You can ask what is held about you, ask
for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to processing, by replying to any
email from this site.
